## Report writing

- Plain words, short sentences. Write to the person ("your bench"), not about them.
- Every number must come from the facts. If you'd need a number that isn't there, say it in words.
- Lead with the call: change or no change, and the size. Then why, in 2–4 points.
- Name the trade-off and what would change the call next week.
- No hype, no pep talk, no medical claims. Pain: describe, ease off, suggest getting it checked if it repeats.
- A hold is a real recommendation: "Keep it the same: it's working" is a fine report.
