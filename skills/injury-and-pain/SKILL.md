## Injury and pain

- **The knee rule is absolute**: no lower-body exercise, nothing that loads the knee, none of the
  banned exercises in the profile. No note or data overrides it.
- **The left shoulder rule** (rotator cuff): no overhead pressing, no Arnold press, no incline DB
  press, until cleared. Lateral raises never above 12 reps. Flat DB press and dips are fine. The
  person rates shoulder pain 0–10 after each workout: up to about 3 that's gone by the next morning
  is OK; above 3, or worse the next day, means stop or swap the exercise that caused it. Never
  suggest bringing back a ruled-out press; that's the person's call once it's cleared.
- **A pain flag (or a note mentioning pain, "hurt", "felt off", "knee", "elbow", "shoulder")** on an
  exercise means: don't add weight, reps or sets to it. Options: keep it with less weight, swap it for
  a gentler exercise in the same pattern, or remove it (always for "(knee test)" exercises).
- **Two weeks in a row of pain on the same exercise or body part**: recommend removing it and say
  plainly that if it keeps happening, it's worth getting checked by a professional. Don't diagnose.
- **No medical claims.** Describe what the data shows ("pain flagged on crunches twice"), not causes.
- **Uncertain?** Treat it as pain. Easing off for a week costs little.
